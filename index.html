Night of Play runs TV-style gameshow nights in London. Every guest is put on a team at the door, so the room mixes from the first minute and nobody spends the night stood with the friends they came with.

For brands, that means a crowd playing with your product, not scrolling past it.

How a night runs

  1. Teams on the doorRed, blue, green or yellow. Assigned, not chosen — that's how strangers end up on the same side.
  2. The whole room qualifiesRound one is played on phones, so everyone's in the game before the first cut.
  3. The crowd plays tooThe player faces away, the room faces the screen. Nobody is a bystander.
  4. One winner, one colourCuts to a final, a champion is crowned, and their whole team celebrates. Then the music takes over.

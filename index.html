Our Story

How many new people did you meet on your last night out?

A city this big shouldn't be this hard to meet people in. We've been there. You arrive with your friends, you stand with your friends, you check your phone, you go home. Nobody meets anybody.

Now imagine leaving a night out with more friends than you arrived with. A new group chat, plans for next time, and a team you'd play with again.

So we built a night that does the introducing for you.

It started at uni, as games nights among friends. Then companies started asking for it, and we took the show on the road for companies like The Crown Estate, the NHS & many more, from corporate Christmas parties to members-only coworking events.

Different rooms, same thing every time: once people are on a team, they talk. Now we've opened the doors to everyone.

When did you last play?

Not scroll. Not watch. Play. If you had to think about it, it's been too long.

It turns strangers into teammatesWhen your team needs you, you talk. By the final, you're celebrating with people you didn't know at the door.
It gets you off your phoneWe'd rather you shout at a screen with strangers than scroll one on your own.

What do you reach for when a conversation dies?

Your phone. At Night of Play, you'll be too busy shouting answers with your team to notice it.

The Gameshow

A live, hosted show built on the gameshow formats everyone grew up watching. Simple rules, no awkward warm-up, and within minutes the guest who “doesn’t do this sort of thing” is shouting answers with everyone else. Three original rounds:

  • Read the room: guess what everyone else said, not what’s right. No specialists, so the quietest person in the room has the same shot as the loudest.
  • Hold your nerve: quick physical challenges against a ticking clock, with the whole room watching. It’s about nerve, not knowledge.
  • Find the word: a handful of letters, a few seconds, and teams racing to build the longest word.

Who We Are

We believe people connect best when they stop trying so hard.

Somewhere along the way, meeting someone turned into work. Profiles to perfect. Openers to rehearse. First dates that feel like job interviews: you both know in five minutes, then spend the evening politely pretending otherwise.

London has never had more ways to meet. So why does it feel so hard?

Think of the people you clicked with instantly. You weren't performing. You were laughing at the same thing, on the same side, lost in the moment. That's when we're most ourselves, and when the magic happens.

We're chasing the version of you that turns up when you forget to be nervous.

So we skip the small talk. We put you on a team.

Hosts run the room. Games break the ice. By round two you're celebrating with someone you met ten minutes ago, and nobody needed an opening line.

A Live Gameshow Experience

The Gameshow

Lights, buzzers and confetti, brought to your event. A host on the mic, your guests on teams, and dozens of games built to pit team against team. Anyone can play. No skill needed, and within minutes the guest who “doesn’t do this sort of thing” is shouting answers with everyone else.

  • Rules hit the screen: every round opens with the rules on the big screen, so guests are playing within seconds.
  • Every round scores: 1st place takes 10 points, 2nd takes 8, 3rd takes 5 and 4th takes 3. Nobody’s out of it until the last game.
  • Top of the table wins: the points are counted, the winners are crowned, and the confetti comes down.

How it works

  1. 01Tell us about your eventYour date, numbers, venue and what you want people to walk away with. One short conversation is enough.
  2. 02We build the show around youWe write the rounds around your group, inside jokes included. You sign them off first.
  3. 03We bring the kitA host, buzzers or phones, the big screen and live scoring, all set up before your guests arrive.
  4. 04Your guests playWe run it from the front, all the way to the winners. You take the credit.

Any size, anywhere

  • Smaller roomsReal gameshow buzzers, the kind you’ve seen on TV. They make a room sound like a studio.
  • Larger roomsEveryone’s phone becomes the buzzer. Nothing to download, and up to 300 devices at once.
  • Online or hybridRemote players buzz, score and win on exactly the same terms as the people in the room.
